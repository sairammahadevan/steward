📋 What it does

STEWARD is your personal document vault. Upload any document — warranty cards, AMC receipts, legal deeds, service records — and it extracts the key information automatically.

Ask plain-English questions about your documents. Get reminded via WhatsApp before things expire (coming in MVP 3).

🔒 Privacy

Your document files stay on your machine. Only the text content is sent to the Anthropic API during processing — and that's deleted from their servers within 7 days.

Never used for AI training. Anthropic privacy policy →

🛠 Tech stack

  • Python · Flask · SQLite
  • Claude API (Anthropic Haiku / Sonnet)
  • PyMuPDF · python-docx
  • Runs entirely on your laptop

🗺 Roadmap

  • ✅ MVP 1 — Upload & extract metadata
  • ✅ MVP 2 — Ask questions about documents
  • 🔲 MVP 3 — WhatsApp alerts for due dates
